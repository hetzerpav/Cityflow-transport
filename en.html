Our transport services

CityFlow Transport is a transport company based in Amstelveen. With our Volkswagen Crafter we move everything that doesn't fit in your car, safely and for a fixed price. We speak English, Dutch and Arabic.

Furniture delivery

Sofas, wardrobes, beds and tables. Including deliveries from furniture stores and IKEA.

Marktplaats pickups

Bought something on Marktplaats or Facebook Marketplace? We collect it from the seller and bring it to you.

Small moves

Student room, studio or a few large items. With an extra helper if you need one.

Appliances

Washing machines, fridges, dryers and dishwashers moved safely and carried inside.

Scooters & motorbikes

Scooters, motorbikes and (e-)bikes strapped down and transported safely.

Pallets & business

Pallet transport, stock and deliveries for companies and online shops.

Building materials

Building materials, tools and machinery delivered to the site or your home.

Our van

We drive a Volkswagen Crafter with a cargo space of about 420 x 200 x 195 cm (L x W x H), roughly 16 m³. It fits a full sofa, a large wardrobe, appliances or the contents of a student room. For heavy items we can bring a second person, so you don't have to lift anything.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quote?

Send the pickup and delivery address, the date and a photo or description of the items on WhatsApp. You'll quickly receive a fixed price.

Do you help with lifting and carrying?

Yes. For heavy or large items we can bring a second person to help carry.

How much fits in the van?

Our Volkswagen Crafter has a cargo space of about 420 x 200 x 195 cm (L x W x H), enough for a sofa, wardrobes, appliances or a small move.

Do you pick up Marktplaats purchases?

Yes. We collect your purchase from the seller and deliver it to your home, also outside Amstelveen and Amsterdam.

Which area do you cover?

From Amstelveen we cover Amsterdam and the whole region, and on request the rest of the Netherlands.
